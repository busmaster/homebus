fuse settings for atmega32u4 (see do8_bootloader/main.c)
extended fuse byte:
0xf8 (changed: hwbe disabled, bod level to 000: 4.0 .. 4.5 V)
fuse byte high:
0xd8 (changed: JTAG disabled, BOOTRST to bootloader)
fuse byte low:
0xdd (changed: ckdiv8 disabled, cklsel to 1101: 3-8 MHz crystal with bod enabled)

fuses
E:f8 H:d8 L:dd
